Kayan Studio

Kayan Studio makes animated explainer videos for YouTube.

Channels

  • Unbored Facts: how machines and megaprojects work, in 3D animation.
  • Ripple You: animated "What If" stories.

Video Studio Uploader

Video Studio Uploader is a private tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own YouTube channels, and to read basic analytics for those channels. It is not offered to the public.
